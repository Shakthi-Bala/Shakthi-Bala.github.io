## The problem

A planar homography can align overlapping views, but predicting a full 3×3 matrix directly introduces scale redundancy. A four-corner representation instead describes the mapping through eight displacement values, which provides a compact regression target.

## Implementation

- **Synthetic supervision.** Samples 128×128 grayscale crops, perturbs their four corners, computes a perspective transform, and generates a second patch from the warped image.
- **Normalized inputs and targets.** Stacks both patches as two channels, scales image intensities, and normalizes corner offsets for training.
- **A convolutional regressor.** Uses convolution, batch normalization, ReLU, pooling, and dropout before fully connected layers that predict eight corner displacements.
- **Supervised training.** Optimizes mean squared error with momentum SGD, evaluates a validation loader, and saves the best validation checkpoint as well as the final model.

The implementation extends the WPI **MyAutoPano** course starter and retains its source attribution. The implemented training objective is supervised corner regression; the starter's unsupervised and panorama-blending placeholders are not presented as completed features.

## Reproduction and scope

Supply the training and validation image directories expected by the wrapper before launching training. The public repository does not include a trained checkpoint or a held-out accuracy report, so this case study documents the implemented pipeline without claiming measured model performance. The GIF verifies the data-generation step only.
