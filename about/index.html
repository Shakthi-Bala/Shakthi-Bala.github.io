  1. Jan 2026 – Present

    Current

    Worcester Polytechnic InstitutePeAR Lab

    Student Researcher

    Worcester, Massachusetts, USA

    • Designed a 24 g thermal–ultrasound sensing package for a 148 g flapping-wing MAV, targeting navigation in snow, smoke, and darkness.
    • Developed ThermalUSFusionNet, a FiLM-conditioned encoder–decoder that fuses thermal frames and ultrasonic A-scans to estimate multi-scale metric depth and uncertainty up to 7 m, with BerHu reconstruction, Ajna attenuation, scale-invariant, and gradient-matching losses.
    • Built a synchronized ultrasound, thermal, and RGB-D capture rig with a heated calibration checkerboard. Curated a 70/15/15 dataset split and used recorded in-flight wing noise to augment clean A-scans.
    • Deployed the stack on a Raspberry Pi Zero 2W at 18 Hz and validated it across 6+ indoor scenes and 4 outdoor sites, including challenging materials such as acrylic, aluminum foil, and matte black surfaces.
    • Developed a velocity-based teacher–student RL navigation policy to address the local-minima failures of potential-field planners.

    Sensor fusion · PyTorch · Depth estimation · Embedded deployment
